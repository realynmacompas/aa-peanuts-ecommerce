FAQs

About A&A Peanuts

What is A&A Peanuts?
A&A Peanuts is a proudly local business that offers high-quality, affordable peanut snacks. Perfect for merienda, parties, or reselling!
Where are your products made?
All our products are freshly made in province of Laguna, Calamba City, ensuring every bite is flavorful and safe.


Ordering and Shipping

How can I place an order?
You can order directly through our website, just add items to your cart and proceed to checkout.
What are your payment options?
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) and GCash for a smooth and easy transaction.
How long does delivery take?
Orders usually arrive within 2–5 business days, depending on your location.
Do you offer same-day delivery?
Currently, same-day delivery is available for select areas. Location covered are within the province of Laguna.


Product & Packaging

How much do your products cost?
Our snacks start at just ₱30 each! Affordable, delicious, and perfect for all ages.


Reseller Program

How can I become a reseller?
You can become a reseller by simply buying 100 or more packs of our products! You will have a great opportunity to earn money and get exciting discounts from your purchase. What are you waiting for?
Is there a minimum order requirement for resellers?
Yes, the minimum order for resellers is 100 packs. This ensures you have enough stock to start your sales.


Support

How can I contact you?
You can message us on our Facebook page or email us at aapeanuts@gmail.com.
What if I received the wrong item or my order is incomplete?
Don’t worry! Just send us a message with your order number, and we’ll fix it as soon as possible.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, A&A Peanuts started as a humble passion project by two siblings with only ₱20 peanut packs. What began as a small dream is now a movement to bring affordable happiness to every Filipino snack lover.

Our Mission

To deliver affordable, mouth-watering, high-quality peanut snacks that bring joy, pride, and value to Filipino homes, while supporting local farmers and empowering small entrepreneurs.

Our Vision

To become a household name in Filipino snack culture, blending unbeatable flavors with heart-centered values that uplift communities nationwide.
